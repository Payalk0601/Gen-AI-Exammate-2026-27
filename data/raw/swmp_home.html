Solid Waste Management (SWM) Rules, 2026

The Solid Waste Management Rules, 2026, were notified by the Ministry of Environment, Forest and Climate Change (MOEF&CC) on 27th January 2026, and came into force with effect from 1st April 2026.

These Rules establish a comprehensive regulatory framework for scientific and environmentally sound management of solid waste.

The Rules delineates the duties and responsibilities of various stakeholders involved in solid waste management, including the Urban Local Bodies (ULBs), Rural Local Bodies (RLBs), State Government Departments, State Pollution Control Boards (SPCBs)/Pollution Control Committees (PCCs), Waste Processing and Disposal Facilities, Bulk Waste Generators (BWGs), and other concerned agencies.

Key obligations that shape everything

Segregate at source, register with your local body, and account for what you generate.

Four-stream segregation at source

Wet, dry, sanitary and special care waste segregated at source and handed over separately to authorised waste collectors.

Extended Bulk Waste Generator Responsibility

BWGs to procure EBWGR certificates from local bodies for undertaking environmental sound waste management.

Regulatory Framework

Governing urban and rural local bodies with responsibility for infrastructure deployment, operational efficiency and compliance reporting.

Waste Processing and Disposal Facilities

Register as Facility
Refused Derived Fuel (RDF)

Refused Derived Fuel (RDF)

Facility where fuel is derived from combustible waste fraction of solid waste like plastic, wood, pulp or inorganic waste, other than chlorinated materials, in the form of pellets or fluff produced by drying, shredding, dehydrating and compacting of solid waste.

Material Recovery Facility (MRF)

Material Recovery Facility (MRF)

Facility where solid waste other than wet waste and horticulture waste can be temporarily stored to facilitate segregation and sorting of collected waste and transfer of recyclables and non-recyclables to authorised recyclers or waste processors.

Material Recovery Facility (MRF)-cum-Refused Derived Fuel (RDF)

MRF-cum-RDF

Facility combining the functions of a Material Recovery Facility and an RDF plant. Registration of MRF-cum-RDF plants is specifically referenced under Rule 11 of rules.

Compressed Biogas (CBG)/Biogas Plant

CBG/Biogas Plant

Facility which entails enzymatic decomposition of the organic matter by microbial action to produce methane rich biogas.

Compost Facility

Compost Facility

Facility which uses a controlled process involving microbial decomposition of organic matter.

Waste to Energy (WtE)

Waste to Energy (WtE)

Facility using solid waste for generation of energy including thermal treatment for generation of electricity.

Integrated Solid Waste Management Facility

Integrated SWM Facility

Facility wherein the solid waste management processes namely segregation, recovery, storage, collection, recycling, processing, treatment or safe disposal are carried out.

Co-Processing Facility

Co-Processing Facility

Facility using non-biodegradable and non-recyclable solid waste having calorific value exceeding 1500 kcal/kg as raw material or as a source of energy or both to replace or supplement natural mineral resources and fossil fuels in industrial processes.

Common Biomedical Waste Processing Facility

Common Biomedical Waste Processing Facility

Facility for the management of sanitary waste, special care waste excluding biomedical waste covered under the Biomedical Waste Management Rules, 2016

Sanitary Landfill

Sanitary Landfill

Facility for final and safe disposal of residual solid waste and inert waste on land in a facility designed with protective measures against pollution of ground water, surface water and fugitive air dust, wind-blown litter, bad odour, fire hazard, animal menace, bird menace, pests or rodents, greenhouse gas emissions, persistent organic pollutants slope instability and erosion.

Incinerator Facility

Incinerator Facility

A facility that thermally treats solid waste through controlled combustion (incineration) to reduce waste volume and/or recover energy, and includes all associated infrastructure such as waste feeding systems, combustion chambers, air pollution control systems, ash handling systems, and monitoring equipment.

Waste Collection Agency

Waste Collection Agency

Facilities engaged in collection and transportation of solid waste. To register, facilities to kindly contact their ULB/RLB.

Event Intimation

Are you organising an event or gathering of more than 100 persons at any unlicensed place? If yes, register your event to intimate your local body at least 3 working days in advance.
