## If something fails during the real demo
- Model quota used up or no internet: ask a question you already asked (cached answers still work), then show the tests, the README results and the 2016 vs 2026 toggle.
- UI will not start: show the API at `/docs` and answer one question there.
- Never apologise at length. Say what happened in one sentence and move to the next point.

## Final check before the interview
- Pitch said aloud twice without notes
- Hardest-problem story told once in under 2 minutes
